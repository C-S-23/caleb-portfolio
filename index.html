03 — Approach

How I work, in three moves.

01

Listen

The frontline taught me to slow down and hear what people actually need — not what they first say. Same goes for a brief, a bug, or a design.

02

Build

Clean, semantic HTML. CSS that respects the user's device. JavaScript that does only what it needs to. No clever for the sake of clever.

03

Refine

Ship, then sand. Tighten spacing, fix the tab order, polish the edge cases. Care shows up in the second pass, not the first.
